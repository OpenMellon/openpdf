# Code of Conduct

OpenPDF follows the [Go Community Code of Conduct](https://go.dev/conduct), adapted below for this project.

## Values

These are the values we want people in this project to hold:

- **Be friendly and welcoming.**
- **Be patient.** People have different communication styles, and not everyone writes English as a first
  language.
- **Be thoughtful.** Productive communication takes effort. Think about how your words will be read, and remember
  that sometimes it is best not to comment at all.
- **Be respectful.** In particular, respect differences of opinion.
- **Be charitable.** Read other people's arguments in good faith and do not look for reasons to disagree. When you
  do disagree, try to understand why.
- **Avoid destructive behaviour:** derailing (staying on topic matters, and off-topic conversation belongs
  elsewhere), unconstructive criticism (do not merely decry the current state of affairs; offer, or at least
  solicit, suggestions for improvement), snarking, discussing potentially offensive or sensitive issues that are
  off-topic, and microaggressions.

People are complicated. Each of us has bad days and everyone makes mistakes. When someone has made a mistake, we
should assume they meant well and help them do better, rather than punishing them.

## Our pledge

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps create a positive environment includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces - issues, pull requests, Discussions, code review and any other
channel run by the project - and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to GitHub:

- On a specific comment, issue, pull request or discussion, use the **...** menu and choose **Report content**.
- For anything else, use <https://github.com/contact/report-abuse>.

GitHub's Trust & Safety team reviews these reports in confidence, and the maintainers moderate project spaces
directly. The maintainers will respond to breaches of this code in a way they judge appropriate
to the circumstances, which may include a warning, removing content, or a temporary or permanent ban
from the project. Anyone who does not follow this code in good faith may face such consequences.
